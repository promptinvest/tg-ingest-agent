Generic undo records the latest supported actual mutation for one chat for ten
minutes. It binds stable identities and exact expected state; it does not resolve
targets again through today's display positions. Inverse is all-or-none and is
consumed once, with an append-only compensation event. Journal payloads/dates and
note lifecycle fields restore exactly; issued note numbers, categories and
original feedback/history remain. Changed targets and overdue rearming refuse
restoration. A recurring deferral's new unchanged echo is cancelled without
moving its series anchor or deleting history. Original unsent success replies are
bound to the action so they cannot arrive after undo as stale assertions; sent
and ambiguous delivery evidence remains. Source deletion/purge removes affected
saved inverse content.

no live task approval and no replied/quoted-card binding. Existing «отмени»,
«верни как было» and «не то» meanings are preserved. Unrelated commands retain
their own behavior; the record represents the latest supported mutation, not a
general transaction history.
